## Agile VS Waterfall

| Agile | Waterfall |
|---|---|
|iterative approach: break down into specific features, tackle under a sprint & take frequent feedback | linear, structured approach: steps completed in sequential order|
|adaptive product roadmap| requirements -> design -> implementation -> verification -> maintenance |
|more collaboration, continuous integration but lack of comprehensive documentation|clear scope & documentation -> communication, timeline, key milestones & easier for new joiners|
|can be difficult to coordinate across multiple teams| difficult to outline all requirements at the beginning -> gaps in documentation|
|lower cost, higher satisfaction (frequent feedback & less customer risk)| minimal customer collaboration -> costly changes if not meeting expectations|
|code defects inform future software design| issues & bugs reported later could have informed an alternative program architecture|

## Product Backlog
- an ordered list of all work a team could do ranked by value & urgency, continuously refined & used to feed planning
- build, prioritize & maintain work separately from the active sprint

|Product backlog|Sprint backlog|
|---|---|
|hold everything the team might do, ordered by priority but not yet promised to any sprint|selected items committed to finishing in a sprint|

- **Importance**: clear priorities, faster planning, less waste, shared visibilities

### How to build & core elements

|Write clear backlog items| common format: user story: As a **role**, I want **outcome** so that **benefit**|
|Prioritize the backlog| MoSCoW sort: must-have, should-have, could-have, won't-have|
|| Effort-value matrix: work VS value|
